ResearchOS Backup privacy policy

ResearchOS Backup is a personal tool used only by its owner.

What it accesses

With the Google Drive scope drive.file, it can see, create, change and delete only the files it uploaded itself. It cannot see any other files in your Google Drive.

How data is used

Backup files are uploaded to the owner's own Google Drive and read back to verify their name, size and checksum. No data is sent to anyone else, sold, shared, or used for advertising. The sign-in token is stored only in the owner's local Mac Keychain.

Removing access

You can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com under Security, third-party access, ResearchOS Backup.
